# Name directions — body, garment and ink

1. **The Worn Line** — a drawn line, a tattoo, a seam and the silhouette of a body. The strongest poetic direction: intimate and expressive without limiting the app to one medium.
2. **Ink & Weft** — joins mark-making with the structure of cloth; concise and tactile.
3. **Skin & Selvedge** — the body's surface and the finished edge of fabric; best for an atelier identity.
4. **Form & Fibre** — body structure, garment construction and the lines that connect them; calm and architectural.
5. **Trace & Drape** — drawing, tracing the body and shaping fabric; a more accessible, process-oriented name.
6. **The Mark We Wear** — treats tattoos and clothing as a shared expression of identity; strongest as an editorial name or tagline.

Possible tagline for The Worn Line: **Draw on form. Shape what is worn.**

These are naming concepts, not availability-checked names. No branding or application identifiers have been changed.
